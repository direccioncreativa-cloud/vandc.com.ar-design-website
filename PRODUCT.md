# Van D.C. — Product context

## Purpose

Van D.C. is the bilingual portfolio and commercial home of an Argentine visual artist. The site must present the work with the restraint of a contemporary gallery while making inquiries, commissions, collaborations and artistic experiences easy to understand and initiate.

## Primary audiences

- International collectors, especially in the United States.
- Galleries, curators and cultural institutions.
- Interior designers, hospitality brands and companies seeking art or experiences.
- Spanish-speaking collectors and collaborators in Argentina and Latin America.

## Brand character

Intimate, expressive, refined, warm and self-possessed. The experience should feel authored by an artist, not generated from a generic portfolio template.

## Experience principles

1. The artwork leads; interface decoration remains quiet.
2. Spanish and English are equal editions of one site, not separate products.
3. Language is editorial and natural, never a literal machine translation.
4. Every page offers a clear next step without becoming commercially aggressive.
5. Motion supports attention and remains optional; content is always visible.
6. Accessibility targets WCAG 2.2 AA.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI/startup styling, neon, glassmorphism and ornamental effects.
- Corporate SaaS language or excessive conversion patterns.
- Two independently maintained language versions.
- Hidden content that depends on JavaScript animation to become visible.

## International launch definition of done

- Complete structural and visual parity between Spanish and English.
- Correct reciprocal language routing, document language and localized metadata.
- No broken internal links, missing images or untranslated interface content.
- Responsive, keyboard-accessible and production-quality across current mobile and desktop browsers.
- Maintainable content and route architecture with automated validation.
