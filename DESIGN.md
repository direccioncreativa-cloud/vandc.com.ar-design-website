# Van D.C. — Design system

## Direction

Editorial contemporary gallery: generous space, strong serif display typography, restrained sans-serif interface text, warm mineral neutrals, dusty rose and antique gold accents. Photography and artwork remain the visual focus.

## Core tokens

- Canvas: warm ivory `#FAF7F2`.
- Paper: blush-tinted neutral `#F7EEE8`.
- Ink: charcoal `#121212`.
- Brand accent: old rose `#744247`.
- Secondary accent: antique gold `#C99B63`.
- Muted copy: smoke `#6D625F`.
- Hairlines: low-contrast warm borders.

## Typography

- Display and artwork titles: editorial serif, tight tracking, compact leading.
- Navigation, labels and metadata: Satoshi, uppercase with measured letter spacing.
- Body: Satoshi, comfortable line length and 1.6–1.8 leading.

## Layout

- Wide editorial containers with consistent responsive gutters.
- Section rhythm uses a small set of reusable vertical spacing values.
- Image ratios reflect the artwork; avoid arbitrary cropping in detail views.
- Primary touch targets are at least 44px where practical.

## Interaction

- Focus states are always visible.
- Hover is additive and never the only way to discover an action.
- Content renders visible by default. Entrance motion uses progressive enhancement and respects reduced-motion preferences.
- Artwork lightboxes close by visible button, outside click and Escape, and restore focus.

## Localization

The two languages share components, layout and content records. Copy, route segments and metadata are localized data. Artwork keeps its original Spanish title with an English rendering where editorially useful.
